key growth drivers in western suburbs

🚑 metro line 2A & 7 impact

Interconnected metro lines running along Link Road and Western Express Highway have compressed travel times between Dahisar and Andheri to under 40 minutes, boosting tenant preference for areas like Malad and Kandivali.

🌳 coastal road extension to kandivali

The northern extension of the Mumbai Coastal Road toward Kandivali is expected to ease vehicular bottlenecks along SV Road, providing direct high-speed connectivity to South Mumbai and Bandra Worli Sea Link.

🏢 redevelopment of housing societies

Older cooperative housing societies in IC Colony, Shimpoli, and Evershine Nagar are undergoing redevelopment under Section 79A of MCS Act, delivering modern multi-storey towers with podium parking and clubhouse amenities.

💼 commercial office clusters

Commercial concentration in Mindspace Malad, Nesco IT Park Goregaon, and Veera Desai Road Andheri provides continuous residential demand from IT, BFSI, and media professionals.

understanding ready reckoner vs market value

The government of Maharashtra publishes annual Annual Statement of Rates that establish the minimum floor value for stamp duty calculation.

Stamp Duty Rule: If the agreed market transaction price is higher than the Ready Reckoner valuation, stamp duty is levied on the actual market value. If the agreed market price is lower, stamp duty is calculated on the higher Ready Reckoner value.

Income Tax Section 50C & 56 2 x: Any variance beyond 10% between the actual agreement consideration and the Ready Reckoner valuation may attract additional capital gains tax for the seller and income tax scrutiny for the buyer.
