Mumbai Market Insights — Q1 2025

🚇

Metro Effect — Line 2A & 7

Properties within 500m of Metro Line 2A (DN Nagar–Dahisar) have appreciated 18–22% since line opening. Malad West and Goregaon are biggest beneficiaries. Expect further 10–15% uplift as ridership grows.

🏗️

New Supply Tight — Prices Rising

New project launches in western suburbs are down 12% YoY due to land scarcity and approval delays. This constrained supply, combined with strong demand, is driving above-average price appreciation in 2025.

💰

Rental Yields Improving

Rental yields in Malad and Goregaon have improved from 2.8% to 3.2% over the past 12 months, driven by IT/ITES employment growth and reduced vacancy rates. Commercial yields remain stronger at 6–8%.

👥

End-User Demand Dominant

78% of buyers in western suburbs are end-users (not investors), creating more stable demand. First-time homebuyer incentives and home loan rates stabilising at 8.5–9% are sustaining healthy transaction volumes.

🌍

NRI Investment Up 35%

NRI investments in Mumbai real estate have surged 35% in FY2024–25, buoyed by favourable exchange rates and strong returns vs global real estate. Premium projects in Andheri and Bandra see highest NRI interest.

🔮

12-Month Outlook: Positive

We anticipate 8–12% appreciation in Western Mumbai through 2025–26. Key drivers: infrastructure completion (coastal road, Trans-Harbour Link), home loan stability, and Mumbai's growing tech employment base.
