🏠 About This Property

Welcome to Skyline Heights Residency — a premium 3 BHK apartment in the heart of Malad West's most sought-after micro-market. This thoughtfully designed home offers the perfect blend of modern living, natural light, and urban convenience.

Located on the 7th floor of a well-maintained 15-storey private building, the apartment enjoys excellent cross-ventilation and partial open views. The layout is highly functional — with a spacious living-dining area, a modern modular kitchen with utility area, 3 well-proportioned bedrooms, and 2 fully-tiled bathrooms.

The society offers 24-hour security, reserved car parking, a children's play area, and a well-equipped gymnasium. Located just 8 minutes from Malad railway station and 5 minutes from the Link Road, connectivity is excellent.
