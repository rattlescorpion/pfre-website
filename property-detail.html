🏠 About This Property

Welcome to Skyline Heights Residency — a premium 3 BHK apartment in the heart of Malad West's most active residential corridor. This thoughtfully designed flat offers the ideal balance of natural cross-ventilation, abundant daylight, and modern city living.

Situated on the 7th floor of an established 15-storey private cooperative housing society, the apartment features an expansive living-dining hall, a modular kitchen fitted with premium Hettich fixtures, 3 quiet bedrooms, and 2 fully tiled modern bathrooms with branded sanitaryware.

Residents enjoy 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance, 1 reserved covered car parking slot, dual high-speed elevators, and a children's play area. With Malad Railway Station just 8 minutes away and Link Road within 5 minutes, connectivity across Mumbai is seamless.
