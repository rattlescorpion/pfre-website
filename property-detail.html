🏠 About This Property

Welcome to Skyline Heights Residency — a premium residential apartment in the heart of Malad West's most active residential corridor. This thoughtfully designed flat offers the ideal balance of natural cross-ventilation, abundant daylight, and modern city living.

Situated on an established floor of a high-rise private cooperative housing society, the apartment features an expansive living-dining hall, a modular kitchen fitted with branded fixtures, quiet bedrooms, and fully tiled bathrooms.

Residents enjoy 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance, reserved covered car parking, high-speed elevators, and society amenities. With Malad Railway Station, S.V. Road, and Metro Line 2A nearby, connectivity across Mumbai is seamless.
