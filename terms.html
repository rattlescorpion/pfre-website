2. scope of real estate advisory representation

Our firm acts as an intermediary property advisor providing the following services across Mumbai Western Suburbs:

  • Identification, curation, and physical walkthrough coordination of residential and commercial properties.
  • Market rate benchmarking, ready reckoner valuation analysis, and developer negotiation.
  • Verification of thirty-year chain title deeds, society share certificates, deemed conveyance status, and municipal sanction plans.
  • Facilitation of doorstep biometric leave and license agreement registration through the Maharashtra government electronic portal.
  • Home loan documentation coordination with scheduled commercial banking partners.

Property Finder Real Estate functions as an advisor and facilitator. We do not construct real estate developments, hold title ownership in developer projects, or guarantee construction completion deadlines committed by builders.

5. leave and license agreement registration protocols

Under Section 55 of the Maharashtra Rent Control Act 1999, registration of tenancy agreements is a statutory requirement. Property Finder Real Estate facilitates e-registration using Department of Registration and Stamps interfaces.

Parties must ensure that provided Aadhaar details, PAN cards, property electricity consumer numbers, and witness identities are authentic and valid. Our firm does not accept liability for delays resulting from government portal downtimes, Aadhaar biometric server timeouts, or document rejections by the Joint Sub-Registrar due to discrepancies in party records.

Government stamp duty and statutory registration fees are paid directly to the state revenue account via eSBTR or GRAS challans and are strictly non-refundable once the government token is generated.
