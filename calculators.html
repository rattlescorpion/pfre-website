Pro Tips

Smart Buying Tips for Mumbai

💡

Keep EMI Under 40% of Income

Most banks approve loans where the total EMI (existing + new) is under 40–50% of your net monthly income. Stay within this to get the best rates.

👩

Register in Wife's Name

Female property owners pay 1% less stamp duty in Maharashtra — saving ₹1 lakh on a ₹1 crore property. Joint registration (Male + Female) gives 0.5% discount.

📅

Budget for Hidden Costs

Apart from stamp duty (5%) and registration (1%), budget for: GST on under-construction properties (5%), society transfer charges, and legal fees.

🏦

Compare Loan Rates

A 0.5% difference in interest rate on a ₹1 Cr loan over 20 years saves over ₹7 lakhs in total interest. Always compare at least 3–4 banks before finalising.

📈

Commercial > Residential Yield

Commercial properties in Mumbai typically yield 6–10% p.a. vs 2.5–4% for residential. However, commercial needs larger investment and has higher vacancy risk.

🔑

Pre-Approved Loans Save Time

Get a pre-approved home loan before property hunting — it speeds up negotiations, gives you bargaining power, and closes deals faster in competitive Mumbai.
