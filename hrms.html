📋 HR Policies
🏖️ Leave Policy
• Casual Leave (CL): 12 days / year
• Sick Leave (SL): 8 days / year
• Earned Leave (EL): 15 days / year (accrued at 1.25 days/month)
• Public Holidays: As per Maharashtra State Government
• Maternity Leave: 26 weeks (as per Maternity Benefit Act)
• Carry Forward: Max 15 EL days can be carried to next year
💰 Compensation Policy
• Salary paid on or before 7th of each month
• Incentives: 0.25% of deal value for sales consultant, 0.5% for senior
• PF: 12% of Basic Salary (employer + employee contribution)
• TDS deducted at source as per applicable slab rate
• Annual increment cycle: April each year
🕐 Working Hours
• Mon–Sat: 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM (9 hours including 1 hr lunch)
• Sunday: Off (or compensation on request)
• Late arrival grace: 15 minutes
• Half day: Leave after 1:00 PM or arrive after 2:00 PM
