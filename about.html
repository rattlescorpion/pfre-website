Our Founding Story

Built on Integrity, Verified by Due Diligence

Founded on August 1, 2018, in Malad West, Mumbai, Property Finder Real Estate was established with a singular objective: to eliminate the ambiguity, hidden terms, and misinformation historically associated with property transactions.

What began as a focused consultancy in Jankalyan Nagar and Lower Kharodi has expanded across Mumbai's Western Suburbs—serving buyers, sellers, investors, and corporate tenants across Malad, Goregaon, Andheri, Kandivali, and Borivali.

From initial property inspection and title verification to Leave & License registration, MahaRERA scrutiny, and society handover, we stand beside our clients through every legal and financial phase.
