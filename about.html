Who We Are

Our Story

Property Finder Real Estate was established in 2018 by Mr. Saurav Ganesh Chowdhury with a simple yet powerful mission: to make real estate transactions in Mumbai transparent, trustworthy, and stress-free for everyone.

Starting from a dedicated office in the heart of Malad West, we quickly built a reputation as the neighbourhood's most reliable property consultants. Word spread through consistent results: families found their dream homes, investors made profitable acquisitions, and landlords secured trustworthy tenants.

Over 7+ years, we've assisted thousands of clients in securing ideal residential flats, commercial offices, and high-yield investment properties across Mumbai's western suburbs. Our deep-rooted knowledge of local neighbourhoods — from 1 BHK apartments in Malad to premium corporate complexes in Goregaon and Andheri — sets us apart.

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To empower every client — whether a first-time homebuyer, an experienced investor, or a business owner — with honest market advice, in-depth legal validation, and end-to-end guidance, making their real estate journey seamless, transparent, and rewarding.

We recognize that purchasing or leasing property is among the most consequential financial steps in life. We treat each transaction with the care, precision, and diligence it deserves.

🔭 Our Vision

To be recognized as Mumbai's most trusted, innovative, and accessible real estate consultancy — a one-stop platform where technology meets authentic human expertise. By 2030, we aim to be the premier property consultancy across Mumbai's western suburbs, distinguished for transparency, client-first advocacy, and measurable community impact.

We envision a future where every Mumbai family has access to honest real estate guidance, and no homebuyer ever navigates the property market without a trusted advocate by their side.
