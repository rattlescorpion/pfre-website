our story

redefining Mumbai real estate advisory with legal accountability

Property Finder Real Estate was established on 1 August 2018 in Malad West, Mumbai, under the leadership of proprietor and founder Saurav Ganesh Chowdhury. The firm was built to eliminate the opacity, aggressive speculation, and incomplete documentation common in suburban real estate transactions.

Operating across Malad West, Goregaon East, Andheri West, Kandivali West, and Borivali West, we serve as dedicated client advocates rather than commission brokers. Every residential resale flat, new development unit, and commercial workplace listed in our catalog undergoes rigorous pre-market title scrutiny, ensuring clear cooperative housing society records, verified deemed conveyance, and full MahaRERA compliance.

From initial property evaluation and comparative market analysis to doorstep biometric leave and license execution, stamp duty adjudication, and bank home loan disbursement, our team delivers seamless guidance backed by deep local micro-market insight.

🛡️ zero litigation policy

Every asset is vetted against municipal sanctions, society share certificates, chain of title deeds, and encumbrance registers.

📝 turnkey legal conveyance

Complete facilitation of leave and license deeds, sale agreements, power of attorney filings, and government e-registration.

four foundational pillars of our advisory

How we protect client equity and eliminate risks across Mumbai property acquisitions.

01

rigorous title investigation

We scrutinize the thirty-year chain of title, deemed conveyance orders, index II entries, society no objection certificates, and BMC sanction plans before presenting any listing.

02

accurate carpet pricing

We operate exclusively on genuine RERA carpet area measurements and micro-market comparative transactions, eliminating misleading super built-up loadings.

03

digital regulatory compliance

From online e-registration and Aadhaar biometric authentication to Section 194-IA TDS deduction filings, our workflow adheres to Maharashtra state revenue rules.

04

end-to-end client advocacy

We represent buyers and landlords from the first site visit through negotiation, agreement drafting, stamp duty adjudication, keys handover, and society transfer fees.
