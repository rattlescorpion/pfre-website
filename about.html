Who We Are

Our Story

Property Finder Real Estate was established in 2018 by Mr. Saurav Ganesh Chowdhury with a simple yet powerful mission: to make real estate transactions in Mumbai transparent, trustworthy, and stress-free for everyone.

Starting from a small office in the heart of Malad West, we quickly built a reputation as the neighbourhood's most reliable property consultants. Word spread — not because of advertising, but because of results. Families found their dream homes. Investors made profitable decisions. Landlords found trustworthy tenants.

Over 7+ years, we've assisted hundreds of clients in finding ideal homes, offices, and investment properties across Mumbai's western suburbs. Our deep-rooted knowledge of the local market — from affordable 1 BHK flats in Malad to premium commercial complexes in Goregaon — sets us apart from the rest.

Mission & Vision

🎯 Our Mission

To empower every client — whether a first-time homebuyer, a seasoned investor, or a business owner — with honest advice, in-depth market expertise, and end-to-end support, making their real estate journey seamless, transparent, and truly rewarding.

Our Journey

Building Trust, Year by Year

From our founding in 2018 to where we stand today — every year brought new milestones, new clients, and deeper roots in Mumbai's real estate landscape.

2018

Founded in Malad West

Mr. Saurav Ganesh Chowdhury establishes Property Finder Real Estate from a small office in Malad West, starting with residential sales and rentals.

2019

Expanded to Commercial Properties

Added commercial leasing and sales to our portfolio, helping businesses find offices, retail spaces, and warehouses across Mumbai's western suburbs.

2021

Launched Property Management Services

In response to strong demand from landlords, we introduced full-service property management — tenant sourcing, rent collection, and maintenance.

2023

Digital Presence & Online Listings

Launched our digital platform with 2,400+ verified listings, enabling clients to browse properties online and connect with us through all major social channels.

2025

7+ Years, 1,800+ Clients Served

Today, Property Finder Real Estate is one of Malad West's most trusted names — with over ₹800 Cr in transactions and hundreds of families happily housed.
