Checklist

Documents Required

👤 Identity & Address

  • Aadhaar Card (original + copy)
  • PAN Card (mandatory)
  • Passport / Voter ID / Driving Licence
  • Passport-size photographs (6)

💰 Income Documents

  • Last 3 months salary slips
  • Form 16 (last 2 years)
  • Last 6 months bank statements
  • ITR (last 2 years) — self-employed
  • CA-certified P&L — business owners

🏠 Property Documents

  • Sale Agreement / ATS
  • Title deed / 7/12 extract
  • NOC from builder / society
  • Approved building plan
  • Property tax receipts
  • OC / CC (if ready possession)
