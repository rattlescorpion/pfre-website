The Prestige of Andheri West

Andheri West stands as Mumbai's foremost entertainment, media, and cultural district. With the vital junction of Metro Line 1 (Ghatkopar–Versova) and Metro Line 2A at D.N. Nagar, residents experience frictionless transit to the domestic airport, BKC, and South Mumbai.

Featuring world-class dining, boutique shopping, and vibrant social scenes across Lokhandwala Complex and Versova, real estate in Andheri West commands premium valuations, sustained rental yields, and unmatched status.
