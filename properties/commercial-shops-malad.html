frequently asked questions on Malad commercial retail shops

Commercial activities must comply with the registered bye-laws of the housing society and municipal trade license permissions. High-risk trades, restaurants requiring commercial chimneys, and late-night establishments require specific society NOC approvals.

Outright sale agreements in Mumbai attract six percent stamp duty plus thirty thousand rupees registration fee for properties valued above thirty lakh rupees. Lease agreements attract standard Maharashtra leave and license stamp duty based on total rent and refundable security deposit.

Carpet area represents the net usable floor area bounded within internal walls. MahaRERA mandates that all commercial properties must be sold strictly on the basis of certified carpet area to prevent loading discrepancies.
