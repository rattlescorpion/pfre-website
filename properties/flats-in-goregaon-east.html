why home buyers choose Goregaon East

🌳 aarey forest backdrop and clean air

Goregaon East borders the dense forest expanse of Aarey Milk Colony and Sanjay Gandhi National Park, offering lower ambient temperatures, scenic green vistas, and a peaceful environment compared to typical suburban pockets.

🏢 self contained integrated living

Townships like Oberoi Garden City bring together high-end residential towers, Oberoi International School, Oberoi Mall, and Commerz commercial office towers within walking distance, minimizing transit friction.

🚑 multimodal transit access

Direct access to the Western Express Highway, Goregaon railway station with harbour and western line services, and metro line 7 connects commuters directly to Andheri East, BKC, and Mumbai international airport.

frequently asked questions on buying flats in Goregaon East

Metro line 7 runs along the Western Express Highway from Dahisar East to Gundavali in Andheri East, providing direct interchange with metro line 1 at WEH station and reducing travel time to commercial hubs in Andheri and BKC.
