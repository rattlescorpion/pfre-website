why establish your business in Andheri

🚑 transit infrastructure

Andheri connects directly to both the western line and harbour rail network, metro line 1 from Versova to Ghatkopar, and metro line 2A along Link Road. Terminal 1 and Terminal 2 of Mumbai airport are accessible within fifteen to twenty-five minutes.

🏢 media, fintech & consulting cluster

The micro-market hosts production houses, entertainment headquarters, advertising agencies, legal firms, IT consultancies, and chartered accountancy practices, ensuring strong commercial synergy and talent retention.

frequently asked questions on commercial spaces in Andheri

Commercial boutique offices in Andheri West and East typically start from 250 sq ft carpet for single-cabin consultancies and scale up to 15,000 sq ft floor plates for corporate headquarters in Grade A business parks.

Commercial leases are generally executed for three to five years with a lock-in period ranging from twelve to thirty-six months. Escalation clauses are typically pegged at five percent annually or fifteen percent every three years.

Ready properties with a valid occupancy certificate do not attract GST. Under-construction commercial properties attract twelve percent GST with input tax credit or applicable standard rates depending on builder tax structure.
