Why Buy or Rent in Malad West?

Malad West is one of the most vibrant residential destinations in Mumbai's Western Suburbs. Offering seamless connectivity via Metro Line 2A (Dahanukarwadi and Malad West stations), Link Road, and S.V. Road, the neighborhood blends established infrastructure with premium lifestyle conveniences.

Residents enjoy quick access to major corporate IT hubs like Mindspace, premier shopping centers including Inorbit Mall and Infiniti Mall, and proximity to Marve and Aksa beaches.

Key Localities in Malad West:

Jankalyan Nagar Lower Kharodi Sundar Nagar Mindspace Evershine Nagar Orlem Chincholi Bunder Marve Road
