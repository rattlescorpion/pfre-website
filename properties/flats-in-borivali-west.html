why home buyers choose Borivali West

🚑 premier connectivity nexus

Borivali railway station serves as the northern terminal for outstation trains and fast local trains to Churchgate. Metro line 2A connects along Link Road with stations at Dahisar, Anand Nagar, Kandarpada, Eksar, and Borivali West.

🌳 lush green surroundings & lifestyle

Close proximity to Sanjay Gandhi National Park, Gorai Creek, Veer Savarkar Udyan, and peaceful tree-lined lanes in IC Colony make Borivali West one of the most balanced residential micro-markets in Mumbai.

📜 title due diligence by PFRE

Every apartment listed with Property Finder Real Estate undergoes strict legal checks including verification of society registration, share certificates, municipal completion status, and chain of transfer documents.

🏫 educational & social infrastructure

Renowned institutions such as St. Francis D'Assisi, Don Bosco High School, and Mary Immaculate Girls High School, along with multispecialty healthcare centres, make it an ideal choice for families.

frequently asked questions on buying flats in Borivali West
