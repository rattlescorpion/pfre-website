Understanding TDS on Property Transactions

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) is mandatory on certain property transactions in India. The buyer/tenant is responsible for deducting TDS and depositing it with the government. Non-compliance attracts interest & penalties.

⚠️ Disclaimer: These calculators are for indicative purposes only. Always consult a Chartered Accountant for actual TDS computation, especially for NRI transactions which involve complex surcharge calculations.

Section 194-IA

TDS on Purchase of Immovable Property (Resident Seller)

When a buyer purchases immovable property (other than agricultural land) from a resident seller for ₹50 Lakhs or more, TDS @ 1% must be deducted. Both buyer and seller must have PAN. Form 26QB must be filed within 30 days.

TDS is calculated on higher of Sale Consideration or Stamp Duty Value.

TDS to Deduct
₹1,00,000
Higher of Sale/Stamp Value₹1,00,00,000
TDS Rate1%
TDS Amount₹1,00,000
Net Amount to Seller₹99,00,000
📋 How to Deposit TDS:
1Login to incometaxindiaefiling.gov.in
2Go to e-File → e-Pay Tax → Form 26QB
3Fill buyer & seller PAN, property details, TDS amount
4Pay online via net banking / debit card
5Download Form 16B (TDS Certificate) and give to seller
6Deadline: Within 30 days from end of month of deduction

Section 195

TDS on Purchase of Immovable Property from NRI

When buying property from an NRI, TDS must be deducted on the entire sale price (not just gains). The buyer needs a TAN. TDS rates vary based on holding period and gains, with applicable surcharge and health & education cess of 4%.

Section 194-IB

TDS on Rent — Individual/HUF Tenant

An Individual or HUF (not liable for tax audit) must deduct TDS @ 2% when monthly rent exceeds ₹50,000. TDS is deducted once a year in the last month of the financial year or the last month of tenancy. No TAN required — use PAN. File Form 26QC.

TDS to Deduct (Annually)
₹0
Annual Rent₹0
TDS Rate2%
TDS (Deducted Once Annually)₹0
Net Annual Rent to Landlord₹0
📋 How to Deposit TDS 194-IB:
1No TAN needed — use your PAN as tenant
2Go to TIN-NSDL / incometax.gov.in → Form 26QC
3Fill tenant & landlord PAN, rent details
4Pay via challan online
5Issue Form 16C to landlord within 15 days
6Deadline: 30 days from end of month of deduction

Section 194-I

TDS on Rent — Businesses, Companies & Professionals

Any person (other than Individual/HUF not liable for tax audit) must deduct TDS on rent if annual payment exceeds ₹2,40,000. Applicable to companies, firms, LLPs, and professionals. Monthly deduction. TAN required.
