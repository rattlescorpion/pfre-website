Section 194-IA • Form 141 Schedule B

TDS on Purchase of Immovable Property (Resident Seller)

Applicable when purchasing immovable property (other than agricultural land) from a resident Indian for ₹50 Lakhs or more. Tax is deducted on the higher of the agreed sale consideration or the Ready Reckoner circle valuation.

New Unified Regime Active: Filing is done through Form 141 Schedule B on the Income Tax Portal. Once processed, the seller generates a unified Form 132 TDS Certificate on TRACES (replacing legacy Form 16B). Joint sellers are consolidated in a single schedule.

TDS is calculated on the higher of Sale Consideration or Ready Reckoner Stamp Duty Value.

📋 Procedural Filing Steps (Form 141):
1Log in to incometax.gov.in using Buyer PAN credentials (no TAN needed).
2Go to e-File → e-Pay Tax → New Payment and select Form 141 (Schedule B - Property Transfer).
3Provide complete property address, agreement value, stamp valuation, and seller PAN details.
4Pay online via Net Banking, NEFT/RTGS, or debit card.
5Download Form 132 Certificate from TRACES portal after 4-5 days and furnish to seller.
6Filing Deadline: Within 30 days from the end of the month in which deduction was made.

Section 195 • NRI Property Sale

TDS on Purchase of Immovable Property from NRI Seller

When purchasing property from a Non-Resident Indian, TDS is levied on the entire gross consideration (unless a Lower Deduction Certificate is obtained). Applicable surcharge and 4% Health & Education Cess must be factored in.

New Unified Framework: Form 141 Schedule E facilitates PAN-based remittance without requiring a prior TAN for qualifying residential acquisitions. The seller obtains a consolidated Form 132 Certificate upon processing. Under legacy rules, a TAN and quarterly Form 27Q filing are required.

📋 NRI Compliance Checklist:
1Verify seller residential status and overseas banking documentation.

3In the absence of a certificate, tax must be withheld on the gross sale price.
4Deposit via e-Pay Tax within statutory deadlines to ensure smooth sale deed registration.

Section 194-IB • Form 141 Schedule A

TDS on Residential Rent (Individual / HUF Tenants)

Individuals and Hindu Undivided Families not subject to tax audit who pay monthly rent exceeding ₹50,000 must deduct TDS at 2%. Deduction is executed once per financial year in the last month of the tenancy or FY.

New Unified Framework: Challan Form 141 Schedule A replaces legacy Form 26QC. Landlords receive a unified Form 132 Certificate (replacing Form 16C). Tenant TAN is not required.

Section 194-I • Corporate & Audited Entities

TDS on Commercial / Entity Rent (Businesses, LLPs, Companies)

Applicable to corporate entities, LLPs, and audited business proprietorships paying rent exceeding ₹2,40,000 per financial year. Deduction is executed monthly. A Tax Deduction Account Number (TAN) is required.

Filing Architecture: Business entities remit monthly withholding under standard challans and file quarterly returns. Landlords receive Form 132 / Form 16A from TRACES for their annual income tax filings.
