privacy policy and personal data protection

This governance document describes how Property Finder Real Estate collects, uses, protects, and retains your personal information across our website, consultation desks, and documentation facilities in Mumbai.

home / privacy policy

1. data fiduciary and controller details

Property Finder Real Estate, an Indian sole proprietorship founded by Saurav Ganesh Chowdhury and operating from Jankalyan Nagar, Malad West, Mumbai, functions as the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the Information Technology Act 2000.

Our operations adhere to MahaRERA regulations under license registration A51800000412. We maintain strict safeguards against unapproved disclosure, unauthorized processing, and commercial resale of client information.

2. categories of personal information collected

We collect only necessary information required to facilitate real estate advisory, property inspections, and statutory documentation:

• contact and identification records: Full name, telephone number, WhatsApp contact, email address, and residential mailing address submitted through lead forms or during office visits.
• property transaction specifications: Preferred micro-market locality, budget thresholds, preferred carpet area, family occupancy details, and financing preferences.
• statutory agreement and conveyance records: Aadhaar numbers and PAN details required strictly for government e-registration of leave and license deeds, sale agreements, and property tax verification. Biometric fingerprint information captured during doorstep registration sessions is transmitted in encrypted form directly to the Maharashtra Department of Registration and Stamps and UIDAI servers, and is never stored on our local computers or databases.
• digital telemetry and analytics: Internet protocol address, browser type, device identifiers, referral pages, and session duration captured to improve user browsing experience and catalog navigation.

3. lawful purposes for data processing

Personal data is processed strictly for legitimate real estate business purposes:

  • Coordinating physical walkthroughs and inspection appointments with property owners and developer sales offices.
  • Drafting standardized leave and license agreements and title verification reports.
  • Facilitating home loan eligibility assessments with authorized banking institutions upon explicit client request.
  • Executing statutory police verification intimations for rental occupancies within Mumbai.
  • Responding to direct telephone, email, and WhatsApp communications initiated by you.

4. data retention and storage security

All electronic lead entries and CRM records are stored within secure hosting environments with standard encryption protocols and authenticated role-based access. Physical document photocopies submitted for society transfer approvals are stored in secured file lockers at our registered Malad West office.

Client records are retained for a period necessary to fulfill statutory obligations under MahaRERA, the Maharashtra Stamp Act, and the Income Tax Act, after which digital records are deleted and physical papers securely shredded.

5. cookie usage and client-side storage

Our website uses lightweight local storage keys and session cookies solely to preserve your visual theme preference, remember catalog filter choices, and prevent redundant form submissions. We do not deploy third-party advertising trackers that monitor your activity across external web destinations.

6. your statutory rights as a data principal

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you hold the following rights regarding your personal information:

right of access and summary Request a complete summary of personal records held by our firm.
right to correction and erasure Seek correction of inaccurate contact data or deletion of past inquiries.
right of grievance redressal Register any concern regarding data processing directly with our grievance desk.
right to nominate Designate an authorized representative in the event of unforeseen incapacity.
