Legal Framework

What NRIs Can & Cannot Buy

✅ NRIs Can Buy

  • Any number of residential properties (flats, bungalows, row houses)
  • Commercial properties (offices, shops, warehouses)
  • Under-construction and ready-to-move properties
  • Joint purchase with resident Indian or another NRI
  • Properties through home loan from Indian banks

❌ NRIs Cannot Buy (without RBI approval)

  • Agricultural land
  • Plantation property
  • Farmhouses
  • Properties bought with foreign currency cash (must use banking channels)
  • Properties that have legal disputes or encumbrances

🏦 NRI Home Loan Details

  • Available from most nationalised and private banks in India
  • Loan amount: up to 80% of property value
  • EMI deducted from NRE/NRO account
  • Interest rates: 8.5% to 10.5% p.a. (2025)
  • Tenure: up to 20 years
  • We have tie-ups with SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and Axis Bank for NRI loans

💰 Repatriation of Sale Proceeds

  • Principal amount repatriable up to original investment amount
  • Capital gains can be repatriated after paying applicable taxes
  • Proceeds must be routed through NRE/NRO account
  • Form 15CA/15CB required for repatriation
  • We facilitate CA assistance for repatriation documentation
  • TDS of 20% + surcharge applicable on long-term capital gains
