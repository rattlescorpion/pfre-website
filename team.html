⭐ Founder & Owner
Mr. Saurav Ganesh Chowdhury
Owner / Proprietor / Sole Proprietor

A dedicated real estate professional with 7+ years of deep experience in Mumbai's dynamic property market. Saurav founded Property Finder Real Estate in 2018 with a clear vision: to make property buying, selling, and renting in Mumbai transparent, efficient, and client-focused.

His encyclopaedic knowledge of Mumbai's western suburbs — particularly Malad West, Goregaon, Borivali, and Andheri — has helped hundreds of families and businesses find their perfect properties. Saurav personally oversees every transaction, ensuring each client receives the honest guidance they deserve.
