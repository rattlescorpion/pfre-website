Types of Property Insurance

🏠

Home Insurance (Structure)

Covers the physical structure of your home — walls, roof, floors, and fixed fittings — against fire, natural calamities, and accidental damage.

  • Fire, lightning, explosion
  • Flood, cyclone, earthquake, storm
  • Landslide, rockslide
  • Accidental damage to structure
  • Aircraft damage, riots, strikes
  • Subsidence and landslip
₹3,000–₹8,000per year (₹50L cover)
Get Quote
🛋️

Home Contents Insurance

Protects your belongings inside the home — furniture, electronics, jewellery, appliances, and valuables — against theft, fire, and damage.

  • Theft and burglary
  • Fire and allied perils
  • Electronic equipment damage
  • Jewellery and valuables cover
  • Portable equipment (laptop, camera)
  • Accidental damage to contents
₹2,000–₹5,000per year (₹20L cover)
Get Quote
🏢

Comprehensive Home Insurance

Best value — covers both structure and contents in a single policy with enhanced benefits including alternate accommodation and personal liability.

  • Structure + Contents cover
  • Personal accident cover
  • Alternate accommodation expenses
  • Public liability cover
  • Domestic servant accidental cover
  • Pet insurance (optional add-on)
₹6,000–₹15,000per year (₹75L cover)
Get Quote
🔑

Tenant Insurance

For tenants who rent a property — covers your personal belongings and provides liability protection without insuring the building structure.

  • Personal belongings against theft
  • Fire damage to contents
  • Accidental damage liability
  • Loss of rent (if uninhabitable)
  • Temporary accommodation cover
  • Appliance breakdown cover
₹1,500–₹4,000per year (₹10L cover)
Get Quote
🏗️

Under-Construction Property

Protects your investment during construction — covers material, labour, contractor liability, and structural defects during the building phase.

  • Material and equipment damage
  • Contractor all-risk (CAR) cover
  • Third-party liability
  • Fire and natural calamity during construction
  • Workmen compensation cover
  • Post-construction structural defect cover
₹8,000–₹25,000per year (project-based)
Get Quote
🏬

Commercial Property Insurance

Comprehensive protection for offices, shops, warehouses, and commercial complexes — including business interruption and machinery breakdown cover.

  • Fire and allied perils
  • Business interruption (loss of income)
  • Machinery breakdown cover
  • Electronic equipment cover
  • Money and securities in transit
  • Plate glass and signage cover
₹12,000–₹40,000per year (₹1Cr cover)
