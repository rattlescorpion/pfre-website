four-step doorstep registration process

step 1: draft drafting & review

Submit details of owner, tenant, property address, rent, deposit, lock-in period, and fittings. We prepare the standardized draft aligned with Department of Registration and Stamps rules.

step 2: doorstep biometric verification

Our certified registration executive visits your residence or office in Mumbai with UIDAI certified biometric fingerprint scanners and web camera to capture Aadhaar authentication for both parties and two witnesses.

step 3: sub-registrar submission

The electronic agreement is submitted to the Joint Sub-Registrar Office Mumbai. Online stamp duty and registration fees are paid directly to the government treasury via eSBTR or GRAS challan.

step 4: registered Index II delivery

Once verified by the sub-registrar, the officially registered leave and license deed and Index II with digital signature and unique government barcode is delivered via email and WhatsApp within 24 to 48 hours.
