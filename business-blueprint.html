⚙️ Phase 8
Operations & SOPs

Standard Operating Procedures for all key business processes.

📞 SOP-01: Lead Handling

  1. Lead received → auto-assigned to available agent (CRM)
  2. WhatsApp within 15 minutes of lead receipt
  3. Call within 30 minutes (9AM–7PM)
  4. Qualify: Budget, Timeline, Locality, Config, Loan needed
  5. Update CRM stage: New → Contacted
  6. Schedule site visit (within 48 hours)
  7. Share 3–5 suitable property options via WhatsApp
  8. Daily follow-up until converted or marked lost

🏠 SOP-02: Site Visit Process

  1. Confirm site visit via call + WhatsApp 24hr before
  2. Send property details + Google Maps location
  3. Agent arrives 15 minutes early at property
  4. Conduct property walkthrough (30–60 min)
  5. Show neighbourhood amenities (metro, schools, markets)
  6. Address all client queries on-site
  7. Update CRM: Site Visit → Negotiation
  8. Send visit summary + comparison by evening

📄 SOP-03: Booking Process

  1. Verbal agreement confirmed → collect token amount
  2. Draft LOI / Token Receipt (₹50K–₹1L token)
  3. Title search & legal verification (advocate)
  4. Draft Agreement for Sale (within 7 days)
  5. Stamp duty calculation (5% male / 4% female)
  6. Registration at Sub-Registrar office
  7. Loan NOC from builder (if required)
  8. Possession letter & society formalities

💰 SOP-04: Commission Collection

  1. Commission letter signed by buyer & seller at booking
  2. Invoice raised (GST: 18% on brokerage)
  3. 50% commission on agreement, 50% on registration
  4. Bank transfer preferred (NEFT/RTGS)
  5. TDS certificate collected from client (if applicable)
  6. Commission split executed in CRM (agent + company)
  7. Receipt issued with GST breakup
  8. Builder payout reconciliation monthly
