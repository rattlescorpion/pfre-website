Malad West — Mumbai's Best-Kept Secret

Malad West is one of Mumbai's most well-rounded residential suburbs — offering affordable housing, excellent social infrastructure, and rapidly improving connectivity. Our home base since 2018, we know Malad West better than anyone.

From the bustling Inorbit Mall and Mindspace IT Park to the peaceful Aarey Colony fringes, Malad West offers something for every buyer — first-time homebuyers, investors, and families alike.

Goregaon — Mumbai's Entertainment & IT Hub

Goregaon is home to Film City (Bollywood's production capital), the sprawling Aarey Milk Colony (now partly Metro shed), and several large IT parks. It's emerged as one of Mumbai's most dynamic suburbs — attracting young professionals, media personalities, and smart investors.

Andheri — Mumbai's Most Connected Suburb

Andheri is arguably the most well-connected suburb in Mumbai — with Western Railway, Metro Line 1, MIDC industrial zone, the international airport, and proximity to BKC. Divided into East and West by the railway tracks, both sides offer distinct character and strong real estate prospects.

Borivali — Green, Peaceful, Family-First

Borivali is the preferred destination for families seeking spacious homes in a green environment. Flanked by the Sanjay Gandhi National Park and the beaches of Gorai, Borivali offers a rare blend of urban amenities and natural serenity — at some of Mumbai's most affordable prices.
