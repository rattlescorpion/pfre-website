FAQ

Career FAQs

Do I need prior experience?

For telecaller and consultant roles, freshers are welcome. We provide full training including MahaRERA certification support. Enthusiasm and communication skills matter more than experience.

How are commissions paid?

Commissions are paid within 7 days of deal closure and payment receipt. Monthly payouts by the 5th of each month. No delays, transparent calculation in CRM.

Are leads provided?

Yes — company digital marketing generates 100+ leads/month shared with the team. You also build your own referral network. We never ask consultants to cold call randomly.

What is the interview process?

Step 1: WhatsApp your CV → Step 2: 30-min phone screening → Step 3: 1-hr in-person interview with owner → Step 4: Offer letter same day. Total time: 2–3 days.
